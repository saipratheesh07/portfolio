Brand build • End to end

From blank page to self-running content engine

Befit came to me with nothing built. I delivered the full stack: brand kit (Charcoal Navy / Signal Gold, the palette on this very site), a 5-page website, and 4 SEO blog posts targeting "private gym Bangalore" keywords.

Then the real work: a multi-agent AI pipeline that researches topics, writes articles, and publishes to the site autonomously. Content production went from 4+ hours per article to fully hands-off. The site keeps growing while I work on other things.

The lesson: a brand doesn't need a content team, it needs a content system. That's the thinking I bring to every project.
